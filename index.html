About Us

24 Frames a Second, One Story at a Time

24FrameHouse is a photography and videography studio built around a simple idea: every frame should earn its place. The name is a nod to cinema — 24 frames per second — and to the craft of making each one count.

We work across portraits, events, commercials, and music videos, bringing the same attention to light, composition, and story to every project — big or small.
