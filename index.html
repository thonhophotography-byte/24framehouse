About the House

24 Frames a Second, Every Room Has a Story

24FrameHouse is a creative house — four studios sharing one roof, one standard of craft. The name is a nod to cinema: 24 frames per second, and the idea that every single one should earn its place.

Whatever you're making — a fashion story, a film, a live show, or you just need the right gear — there's a room built for it.
